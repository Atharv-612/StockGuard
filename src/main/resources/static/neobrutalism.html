THE RULES OF NEO-BRUTALISM

ANATOMY OF RAW DESIGN

Why make interfaces look like sterile corporate templates when you can make them feel like a tangible zine?

⬛

Thick Black Borders

Every single card, button, and image boasts a hard 3px–4px black border. The bones of the architecture are proudly on display.

OFFSET 8PX
📦

Flat Solid Shadows

Zero blur radii. Zero soft atmospheric glows. Pure solid geometric blocks shifted by exactly 6px to 10px on both X and Y axes.

🎨

Unapologetic Colors

High-voltage electric yellows, neon cyans, magenta pinks, and laser greens colliding boldly against stark dark ink.

TYPE SYSTEM
✍️

Tri-Font Typo Stack

Space Grotesk for bold geometric headlines, Space Mono for precise code, and Caveat for handwritten human notes.
