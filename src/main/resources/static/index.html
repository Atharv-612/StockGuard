Core Capabilities

Built for Real Shop Realities

Everything a neighborhood grocery or dairy owner needs to eliminate stock shrinkage and speed up counter checkout.

Multi-Batch Tracking

Manage separate manufacturing dates, expiry dates, and unit costs for different batches of the exact same product SKU.

Configurable Expiry Radar

Set warning thresholds (e.g., 7 days) and color-coded status badges: Safe, Expiring Soon, and Expired. Never sell expired stock.

WhatsApp Business Cloud Alerts

Receive automatic WhatsApp notifications directly to the owner's phone for low-stock thresholds, expiry warnings, and daily totals.

High-Speed POS Checkout

Rapid counter billing with stock ceiling validation, instant FIFO inventory deductions, and thermal printable receipts.

Sales & Wastage Analytics

Interactive monthly sales charts, product revenue rankings, and financial audits calculating monetary losses from expired goods.

Complete Shop Isolation

Spring Security stateless JWT authentication ensures that every store owner's inventory and revenue data is strictly isolated.
